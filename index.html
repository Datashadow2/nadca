The Law Explained

The Nairobi City County Alcoholic Drinks Control and Licensing Act (NADCA) sets clear rules to protect young people. Here are the key provisions.

🚧 The 300m Rule

No new license can be granted to premises within 300 metres of a nursery, primary, secondary, or other learning institution for persons under 18. A 2022 Nairobi Assembly motion also called for the closure and relocation of existing outlets within this zone.

🔞 Age Restrictions

Section 32(1) prohibits selling, supplying, or allowing access to alcoholic drinks for anyone under 18 years. Penalties include a fine up to Ksh 150,000, imprisonment up to 12 months, or both.

🪪 ID Requirement

Section 32(4) mandates operators to demand a valid ID for age verification before selling alcohol.

📊 Why It Matters

NACADA/KIPPRA surveys found alcohol was the most abused substance among primary and secondary students — with the average user age at 11 years and the lowest at 4 years.

Know Your Rights & Take Action

📝 Petition the Liquor Licensing Committee

Through your estate association, you can formally oppose the license renewal of an outlet that violates NADCA. Petitions are submitted to the County Liquor Licensing Committee.

📞 Report to Authorities

About Us

Voice for Change is a youth-led initiative working to protect young people in Nairobi from alcohol and drug abuse. Guided by the Nairobi City County Alcoholic Drinks Control and Licensing Act (NADCA), we collect reports of violations and channel them to the relevant authorities.
